\begin{abstract}
We present a geometry-and-learning study bridging classical binocular stereo, zero-shot monocular metric depth calibration, streaming video consistency, and thin-lens bokeh rendering under strict zero-test-leakage protocols. First, on all 15 Middlebury \texttt{trainingQ} rectified stereo pairs, 3-way \texttt{StereoSGBM} with a 1~px left-right consistency (LRC) check achieves \textbf{1.4203~px} disparity MAE across \textbf{62.20\%} valid pixels (\textbf{44.0~ms/pair} CPU) and \textbf{4.65~cm} metric depth MAE (\textbf{1.60\%} AbsRel), empirically confirming the $O(Z^2)$ triangulation error law across 1.0--7.5~m while CLAHE + OpenCV guided hole-filling reduces non-occluded MAE from \textbf{10.01~px} to \textbf{4.44~px}. Second, auditing frozen \texttt{UniDepthV2-Small} strictly on the 795 NYU Depth v2 training images reveals three coupled biases: self-predicted focal lengths inflate by $+10.4\%$ ($\mathrm{median}=573.11$~px vs.\ Kinect $519.16$~px, $\rho=-0.534$), internal uncertainty weakly tracks log error (intra-image Pearson $+0.277$, $\mathrm{AUSE}=0.276$), and intra-room contrast compresses ($\beta_0=1.058$). Following five-fold train cross-validation without test peeking, our \textbf{4367-D ScaleProbe-2P} predicts image-wise log-scale and contrast exponent in \textbf{2.18~ms/img} GPU, reducing official 654-image NYU test AbsRel from \textbf{0.1145} raw (and \textbf{0.1020} frozen scalar) to \textbf{0.0821} ($\delta_1=0.9383$, hurt fraction $37.2\%\to 27.5\%$, improving 15/16 room categories and reaching \textbf{0.0816} with a self-calibrated $\hat{K}_{\mathrm{train}}$). Third, attention-sink KV-cache streaming in \texttt{Video Depth Anything-Small} cuts peak VRAM by \textbf{84.8\%} ($8016\to 1219$~MiB) and latency by \textbf{28.9\%} ($60.9\to 43.3$~ms/frame) across three video clips. Finally, combining shared clip intrinsics $K_{\mathrm{clip}}$ with a physical zero-sky intercept ($b=0$) in Huber-IRLS keyframe anchoring cuts outdoor OPW flicker by $4.2\times$ over official \texttt{Metric-VDA-S} ($0.1437\to 0.0343$), restores $P_{95}$ bokeh blur from $2.06$~px to \textbf{6.44~px}, and reduces foreground-to-background halo luminance bleeding by \textbf{63.8\%}. Code: \url{https://github.com/Oliver-ming/Intro_to_CV_project_3}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Recovering physically meaningful metric depth $Z$ from images spans a classic spectrum. Multi-view triangulation offers exact scale via baseline $B$ and focal length $f$, yet fails inside textureless surfaces or half-occluded regions and suffers quadratic $O(Z^2)$ sensitivity with range~\cite{hirschmuller2008sgm,scharstein2014middlebury}. Conversely, modern affine-invariant single-image predictors such as MiDaS~\cite{ranftl2020midas}, DPT~\cite{ranftl2021dpt}, and Depth Anything V2 (\texttt{DAv2})~\cite{yang2024dav2} synthesize sharp edges up to an unknown per-frame scale and shift, inducing temporal flicker in video and requiring ground-truth least-squares alignment before any metric reasoning. Zero-shot camera-aware predictors (\texttt{UniDepthV2}~\cite{piccinelli2025unidepthv2}, Metric3Dv2~\cite{yin2024metric3dv2}, Depth Pro~\cite{bochkovskii2025depthpro}, MoGe-2~\cite{wang2025moge2}) infer metric $Z$ directly from single RGB frames, while video transformers (\texttt{Video Depth Anything}~\cite{chen2025vda}) enforce clip-level coherence.

Under strict deployment protocols---zero test-set alignment on NYU Depth v2~\cite{silberman2012nyu,eigen2014depth} and zero ground-truth scale on unconstrained video~\cite{perazzi2016davis}---two severe bottlenecks emerge cleanly in our measurements:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Single-image metric miscalibration:} When RGB intrinsics are absent, \texttt{UniDepthV2-Small} systematically overestimates Kinect focal lengths ($\mathrm{median}=573.11$~px vs.\ $519.16$~px), shrinking scene scale ($r=0.956$) while compressing dynamic range ($\beta_0=1.058$). A frozen scalar ($0.9563$) leaves substantial residual error ($\mathrm{AbsRel}=0.1020$) and \emph{degrades} $37.2\%$ of test images.
  \item \textbf{Temporal metric inconsistency \& bokeh halos:} Frame-wise \texttt{DAv2-Small} flickers along moving contours, while offline 32-frame \texttt{VDA-Small} consumes $8.0$~GiB VRAM. Na\"ively anchoring relative inverse depth via affine IRLS against per-frame raw intrinsics introduces a nonzero sky intercept ($b>0$), capping sky distance ($105$--$187$~m) and collapsing outdoor defocus blur to $2.06$~px, while naive gather filtering leaks dark foreground struts into bright skies.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Contributions.} Evaluating every stage against exact baselines without test leakage, we make four contributions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Complete Part~1 Stereo Metric Baseline:} Beyond disparity MAE ($1.4203$~px) and valid fraction ($62.20\%$), we visualize left-right invalid masks, benchmark metric depth $Z$ ($4.65$~cm MAE, $1.60\%$ AbsRel), verify $O(Z^2)$ error growth empirically across $1.0$--$7.5$~m, and ablate CLAHE + OpenCV guided hole-filling ($10.01\to 4.44$~px non-occluded MAE).
  \item \textbf{Hierarchical Calibration (\texttt{ScaleProbe-2P} \& $\hat{K}_{\mathrm{train}}$):} Driven by train-only diagnostics on 795 NYU images, a 4367-D ridge probe (2.18~ms/img GPU) lowers test AbsRel from $0.1145\to 0.0821$ ($\delta_1=0.9383$, $\mathrm{SILog}=9.36$, $-28.3\%$) and $0.0816$ with $\hat{K}_{\mathrm{train}}$, generalized under Leave-One-Scene-Out ($0.0853$), feature/error decomposition, and Bayesian soft-fusion of noisy person-height cues ($1.3119\to 0.0873$ voted AbsRel).
  \item \textbf{Streaming Video \& Multi-Clip Metric Consistency:} Benchmarking three clips (\texttt{davis\_rollercoaster}, \texttt{still}, \texttt{moving}) with optical-flow warping error (OPW~\cite{teed2020raft,wang2023nvds}), stationary TAE, 2nd-order jitter, and scale drift, we quantify \texttt{VDA-Small} KV-cache streaming ($-84.8\%$ VRAM, $-28.9\%$ latency), \texttt{RAFT-EMA}, official \texttt{Metric-VDA-Small}~\cite{chen2025vda}, and causal sparse-keyframe anchoring.
  \item \textbf{Physical Zero-Sky Anchoring \& Halo-Free Bokeh:} Enforcing shared $K_{\mathrm{clip}}$ and $b=0$ raises disparity--$1/Z$ correlation ($0.340\to 0.466$), restores $P_{95}$ CoC from $2.06\to 6.44$~px, and our background-visibility premultiplied renderer cuts halo luma bleed by \textbf{63.8\%} on \texttt{rollercoaster} and \textbf{26.1\%} indoors.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work}
\paragraph{Classical \& Learned Stereo.}
Semi-Global Matching (SGM)~\cite{hirschmuller2008sgm} aggregates directional scanline smoothness costs for efficient CPU stereo~\cite{scharstein2014middlebury}, often paired with LRC masking, CLAHE~\cite{zuiderveld1994clahe}, and edge-aware hole filling~\cite{he2013guided,min2014fast}. Recent foundation stereo networks~\cite{wen2025foundationstereo,jiang2025defomstereo,guan2025bridgedepth} narrow sim-to-real gaps across benchmarks~\cite{geiger2012kitti} but sacrifice lightweight CPU execution. We anchor Part~1 strictly on classical 3-way SGBM with explicit LRC invalid maps and metric triangulation.

\vspace{-0.2em}
\paragraph{Monocular Metric Depth \& Scale Calibration.}
Relative predictors~\cite{ranftl2020midas,ranftl2021dpt,yang2024depthanything,yang2024dav2} train on multi-dataset affine-invariant losses, whereas ZoeDepth~\cite{bhat2023zoedepth}, Metric3Dv2~\cite{yin2024metric3dv2}, Depth Pro~\cite{bochkovskii2025depthpro}, UniDepth~\cite{piccinelli2024unidepth,piccinelli2025unidepthv2}, MoGe~\cite{wang2025moge,wang2025moge2}, and DA3~\cite{lin2025da3} predict metric depth or camera intrinsics directly. Prior calibration works adapt depth via camera extrinsics~\cite{zhu2020scalenet}, language-conditioned distributions~\cite{zeng2024rsa}, sparse depth prompts~\cite{lin2025promptda,wang2025priorda}, equivariant FoV canonicalization~\cite{guo2025dac}, diffusion intrinsic refinement~\cite{deng2025dmcalib}, or dataset-specific heads~\cite{zhu2024scaledepth} evaluated across RGB-D benchmarks~\cite{sturm2012tum,silberman2012nyu}. Complementing pixel uncertainty estimation~\cite{ilg2018uncertainty}, we show that frozen \texttt{UniDepthV2} decoder/camera tokens already encode scene scale and intra-room contrast, enabling millisecond closed-form dual-Gram calibration without backpropagation or test-time optimization.

\vspace{-0.2em}
\paragraph{Video Depth Consistency \& Thin-Lens Bokeh.}
Temporally consistent video depth progressed from test-time optimization~\cite{wang2023nvds} and video diffusion (\texttt{DepthCrafter}~\cite{hu2025depthcrafter}, \texttt{RollingDepth}~\cite{ke2025rollingdepth}) to multi-view geometry transformers~\cite{wang2025vggt} and streaming KV-cache architectures (\texttt{VDA}~\cite{chen2025vda}, \texttt{FlashDepth}~\cite{chou2025flashdepth}). Unlike offline 32-frame windows ($8$~GiB), we pair KV-cache streaming with RAFT flow warping~\cite{teed2020raft}, shared-$K_{\mathrm{clip}}$ IRLS anchoring, and occlusion-aware thin-lens bokeh~\cite{peng2022bokehme,sheng2024drbokeh} to prevent dark foreground-into-sky halos.

\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Part 1: Rectified Stereo Geometry \& Range Sensitivity}
Given rectified Middlebury \texttt{trainingQ} stereo pairs $(I_L, I_R)$ and calibration metadata $(f, B, d_{\text{offs}})$ from \texttt{calib.txt}, we run 3-way OpenCV \texttt{StereoSGBM} (SAD window $5\times 5$, $P_1=600$, $P_2=2400$, uniqueness ratio $10$, speckle $100\times 2$), paired with a flipped right-to-left pass accepting disparity $d_L(p)$ only when $|d_L(p) - d_R(p - (d_L(p),0))| \le 1.0$~px and $d_L(p)>0$. Valid disparities convert deterministically to metric depth (m):
\begin{equation}
  Z(p) = \frac{f B}{1000\left(d_L(p) + d_{\text{offs}}\right)}, \qquad
  \left|\frac{\partial Z}{\partial d}\right| = \frac{1000\, Z^2}{f B},
  \label{eq:stereo}
\end{equation}
explicitly explaining why uniform $1$~px matching errors trigger quadratic $O(Z^2)$ metric drift at longer ranges. We further ablate LAB $L$-channel CLAHE illumination normalization~\cite{zuiderveld1994clahe}, an OpenCV pure-boxFilter normalized guided filter~\cite{he2013guided} filling LRC holes over non-occluded regions (\texttt{mask0nocc.png}), and 8-path \texttt{HH} DP cost aggregation.

\subsection{Parts 2 \& 3: Hierarchical NYU Calibration}
All NYU Depth v2~\cite{silberman2012nyu} evaluations resize predictions to $480\times 640$, clip $[10^{-3}, 10]$~m, evaluate inside the official Eigen crop $[45{:}471, 41{:}601]$, and average equally across all 654 official test images with \textbf{zero test-set alignment}. All parameters, scalers, $\kappa$, Ridge $\lambda$, and Bayesian noise variances are fitted strictly on the 795 official training images (\texttt{trainNdxs}).

\vspace{-0.2em}
\paragraph{Train-Only Diagnosis of Zero-Shot Biases.}
Extracting frozen \texttt{UniDepthV2-Small} predictions on the 795 training frames reveals three systematic, physical failure modes:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Camera--Scale Coupling:} In RGB-only mode, \texttt{UniDepthV2} overestimates NYU's Kinect focal length ($519.16$~px) with $\mathrm{median}(\hat{f})=573.11$~px ($\mathrm{IQR}=[558.5,583.8]$, $\sigma=90.9$~px), creating negative correlation between predicted $\log \hat{f}_i$ and optimal per-image median scale $r_i^{\text{med}} = \mathrm{median}_p(G_i/D_i)$ ($\rho = -0.534$, and $-0.482$ for least-AbsRel $r_i^{\text{opt}}$; global median $s_{\mathrm{med}}=0.9563$).
  \item \textbf{Structured Uncertainty Alignment:} Inside training scenes, predicted confidence $\hat{u}(p)$ correlates positively with scale-aligned error $|\log(r_i^{\text{med}} D_i(p)) - \log G_i(p)|$ (intra-image Pearson $+0.277$, Spearman $+0.290$; train-verified sparsification $\mathrm{AUSE}=0.276$), guiding pooled error features.
  \item \textbf{Intra-Room Contrast Compression:} Regressing $\log(G_i/d_{\text{med},i}) = \beta_i \log(D_i/d_{\text{med},i})$ per train frame yields $\mathrm{median}(\beta_i) = 1.058$ ($\mathrm{IQR}=[0.986,1.140]$): raw predictions slightly compress room depth range ($\beta_i > 1$), which a single scalar cannot fix.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{-0.2em}
\paragraph{Hierarchical Formulation (\texttt{ScaleProbe-2P} \& $\hat{K}_{\mathrm{train}}$).}
We construct an interpretable progression: (1) frozen global scalar $s_{\mathrm{med}}$; (2) centered global 2P power-law $(s_0, \beta_0)$; (3) analytic FoV power law $\hat{s}(\hat{f}_i)=\exp(b_0 + b_1 \log\hat{f}_i)$; and (4) \textbf{\texttt{ScaleProbe-2P}}, a dual-Gram $L_2$-ridge linear probe over block-weighted features $\tilde{x}_i = [\sqrt{w_k}\,\phi_k(I_i)]$ extracted solely from frozen \texttt{UniDepthV2-Small} ($\phi_{\text{geo}}\in\mathbb{R}^{15}$ standardized log intrinsics/vertical log-tilt/depth \& uncertainty quantiles; $\ell_2$-normalized ViT CLS $\phi_{\text{cls}}\in\mathbb{R}^{1536}$, spatial patch tokens $\phi_{\text{pat}}\in\mathbb{R}^{2304}$, decoder depth summary $\phi_{\text{dfe}}\in\mathbb{R}^{512}$, plus optional spatial decoder $\phi_{\text{dfe\_sp}}\in\mathbb{R}^{2048}$ and camera head latents $\phi_{\text{cam}}\in\mathbb{R}^{1024}$):
\begin{align}
  \hat{s}_i &= \exp\!\big(w_s^\top \tilde{x}_i + b_s\big), \quad
  \hat{\beta}_i = \operatorname{clip}\!\big(w_\beta^\top \tilde{x}_i + b_\beta,\, 0.80, 1.25\big), \nonumber\\
  \hat{D}_i(p) &= \operatorname{clip}\!\left(\hat{s}_i\, d_{\text{med}, i} \left(\frac{D_i(p)}{d_{\text{med}, i}}\right)^{\hat{\beta}_i},\; 10^{-3}, 10\right),
  \label{eq:probe2p}
\end{align}
where ridge weights $(w_s, b_s)$ and $(w_\beta, b_\beta)$ minimize squared error against train oracle targets $(\log r_i^{\text{opt}}, \beta_i)$, and decoupled regularizations $(\lambda_s, \lambda_\beta)$ are chosen exclusively by 5-fold train AbsRel CV over $\{0.005,\dots,10.0\}$. Selecting strictly by Train CV chooses the 4367-D $(\phi_{\text{geo}}, \phi_{\text{cls}}, \phi_{\text{pat}}, \phi_{\text{dfe}})$ model (0.0865 CV) as our primary single-backbone calibrator, while we report expanded 6927-D \texttt{v2} and cross-backbone \texttt{+DAv2} transparently. Optionally, for uncalibrated fixed-rig deployment ($\hat{K}_{\mathrm{train}}$), we cross-validate one train scalar $\kappa \in \{0.84,\dots,1.00\}$ shrinking median train focal lengths $\tilde{f}_x,\tilde{f}_y$ into $\hat{K}_{\mathrm{train}}=\operatorname{diag}(\kappa\tilde{f}_x, \kappa\tilde{f}_y, 1)$ ($\kappa=0.86$, $\hat{f}_x=492.5$~px). For occasional semantic priors such as noisy \texttt{KeypointRCNN}~\cite{he2017maskrcnn} standing-person heights $s_{\text{person}}$, we replace brittle hard overrides with Bayesian inverse-variance fusion using strictly train-estimated uncertainties $(\sigma_{\text{prior}}, \sigma_{\text{obs}})$:
\begin{equation}
  \hat{s}_{\text{post}, i} = \frac{\sigma_{\text{obs}}^2\, \hat{s}_{\text{prior}, i} + \sigma_{\text{prior}}^2\, s_{\text{person}, i}}{\sigma_{\text{prior}}^2 + \sigma_{\text{obs}}^2}.
  \label{eq:bayes}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Metric Video Anchoring \& Halo-Free Thin-Lens Bokeh}
We evaluate three videos up to 100 frames at max width 640~px (\texttt{davis\_rollercoaster.mp4} containing 70 frames total at 24~fps, plus indoor static \texttt{still.mp4} and dynamic handheld \texttt{moving.mp4} at 100 frames each) under frozen clip-wide $[P_2, P_{98}]$ normalization~\cite{ranftl2020midas,chen2025vda}. Following official \texttt{Video Depth Anything} streaming inference (\texttt{run\_streaming.py}, attention-sink KV cache), we benchmark both relative VDA and official \texttt{Metric-VDA-Small} alongside causal \texttt{RAFT-EMA} forward-backward warping~\cite{teed2020raft} and sparse-keyframe metric anchoring (every $K=15$ frames).

Crucially, naive affine alignment $1/Z \approx a\, d_{\text{vda}} + b$ against uncalibrated per-frame intrinsics exhibits two failure modes outdoors: focal-length jumps distort keyframe scales, while unconstrained $b>0$ bounds infinite sky ($d_{\text{vda}}\to 0$) to finite distance $1/b$, collapsing bokeh blur. We resolve both by (i) pooling keyframe intrinsics via median $K_{\mathrm{clip}}$ so all anchor keyframes share geometry, and (ii) enforcing the physical zero-sky constraint $b = 0$ on outdoor clips via confidence-weighted Huber-IRLS~\cite{huber1964robust}:
\begin{align}
  a^\star &= \arg\min_{a>0} \sum_{t\in\mathcal{K}, p} w_t(p)\, \rho_\delta\!\left(\frac{1}{Z_t^{\text{key}}(p)} - a\, d_t^{\text{vda}}(p)\right), \nonumber\\
  Z_t(p) &= \operatorname{clip}\!\left(\frac{1}{a^\star d_t^{\text{vda}}(p)},\; 0.4,\; 500\right),
  \label{eq:irls}
\end{align}
running both globally offline and causally frame-by-frame via an exponentially smoothed running scale $a_t$ updated only as keyframes arrive. Finally, our bokeh renderer computes physically meaningful one-sided background circle-of-confusion radius
\begin{equation}
  R_{\mathrm{CoC}}(p) = \sigma_{\max}\, \max\!\left(0,\, 1 - \frac{Z_f}{Z(p)}\right)
  \label{eq:coc}
\end{equation}
and gathers background blur using premultiplied background visibility weights $w_{\text{bg}}(p) = \epsilon + (1-\epsilon)\operatorname{clip}((Z(p)-Z_f)/\tau_Z, 0, 1)$, composited under front-to-back foreground alpha $\alpha_{\text{fg}}(p)$ to stop dark in-focus foreground pixels from bleeding into blurred backgrounds~\cite{peng2022bokehme,sheng2024drbokeh}.

\subsection{Cross-Model Comparison \& Bayesian Person Redemption}
To contrast relative vs.\ metric backbones under matched hardware conditions on \texttt{640x360}, \texttt{DAv2-Small} executes in 16.94~ms (417.9~MiB, FP32) or 15.37~ms (343.4~MiB, FP16), yet yields strictly affine-invariant relative disparities requiring GT least-squares shift-scale per test frame (\texttt{ScaleProbe} with \texttt{+DAv2} features hits 0.0828 without test leakage). Meanwhile, incorporating geometric human-height cues via \texttt{KeypointRCNN} (118 voted train frames / 60 voted test frames) illustrates the hazard of brittle semantic overrides: blindly overriding scale with detected person heights collapses 654-image NYU AbsRel from $0.1020$ to \textbf{0.2243} (1.3119 on the 60 voted frames) due to seated/cropped poses ($\sigma_{\text{obs}}=1.6928$ on train). Using strictly train-fitted Bayesian inverse-variance fusion (Eq.~\ref{eq:bayes}, $\sigma_{\text{glob}}=0.0991$, $\sigma_{\text{probe}}=0.0697$) neutralizes outliers to 0.1021 (0.1051 voted) with the global prior and reaches \textbf{0.0852 AbsRel} ($\delta_1=0.9340$, 0.0873 voted) alongside \texttt{ScaleProbe-2P}.

\section{Conclusion}
Under unified zero-test-leakage protocols spanning 15 Middlebury stereo pairs, 1,449 NYU Depth v2 frames, and three multi-domain videos, classical geometry and learned priors prove tightly complementary. In Part~1 stereo, LRC + CLAHE + guided hole-filling delivers sub-5~cm near-range precision yet obeys quadratic $O(Z^2)$ degradation. In Parts~2--3, auditing frozen \texttt{UniDepthV2-Small} solely on NYU train uncovers focal-length--scale bias, intra-room contrast compression, and calibrated uncertainty, enabling a 2.18~ms 4367-D \texttt{ScaleProbe-2P} that drops NYU test AbsRel from $0.1145\to 0.0821$ ($0.0816$ with $\hat{K}_{\mathrm{train}}$, cutting degraded-frame rate from $37.2\%$ to $27.5\%$) and redeems noisy person-height overrides via Bayesian fusion (0.0852). Finally, combining KV-cache streaming \texttt{VDA-Small} ($-84.8\%$ VRAM) with shared-$K_{\mathrm{clip}}$, zero-sky keyframe IRLS ($4.2\times$ lower outdoor OPW than official \texttt{Metric-VDA-S}) enables temporally coherent metric depth and occlusion-aware thin-lens bokeh ($-63.8\%$ halo bleeding).

\paragraph{Limitations \& Future Outlook.}
Our error decomposition shows $\sim 80\%$ of remaining single-image error lies in unpredictable global room scale when architectural height cues are absent, while horizontal tilt ($t_x$) remains unidentifiable from monocular textures ($R^2_{\mathrm{CV}}=-0.011$); incorporating sparse SLAM parallax or inertial scale remains a promising next step.
